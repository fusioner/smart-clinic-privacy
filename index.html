Privacy Policy

Smart Clinic Assistant

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Smart Clinic Assistant is a demonstration application designed to facilitate appointment scheduling and automated communication through WhatsApp using artificial intelligence. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, processed and used when interacting with the application.

This application is currently intended for demonstration and testing purposes. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or emergency medical services. Please do not submit confidential medical information.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with the application, we may process:

  • Your name and WhatsApp phone number.
  • Messages submitted through WhatsApp.
  • Appointment dates and preferred time slots.
  • Appointment booking, cancellation and rescheduling details.
  • Technical information required to operate the service.

3. How We Use Information

  • Responding to appointment-related inquiries.
  • Checking appointment availability.
  • Booking, rescheduling and cancelling appointments.
  • Providing appointment-related confirmations.
  • Testing, maintaining and improving the application.

4. Artificial Intelligence and Third-Party Services

Smart Clinic Assistant uses third-party services to process messages and manage appointment-related activities. These services may include:

  • Meta WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • n8n workflow automation.
  • OpenAI artificial intelligence services.
  • Google Calendar.

Information necessary to perform the requested functions may be transmitted to these service providers and processed according to their applicable privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers as necessary to operate the application or when required by applicable law.

6. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect personal information. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained in appointment records, workflow execution logs, conversation memory and third-party systems as necessary for operational and testing purposes, subject to applicable retention settings and legal requirements.
